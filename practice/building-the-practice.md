# Building the practice

Succession means the practice runs without its first author: others can sell, run and finish an
assessment. Six moves.

1. **Productize.** Three formats with fixed inputs, outputs, acceptance and estimates
   ([offerings.md](../offerings.md)). The kit in this repository is the core of the Assessment
   format.
2. **Roles, not people.** Lead, observability engineer, instrumentation architect, reviewer.
   Succession = a second person in every role, first of all the lead.
3. **Grow people on live work.** Every new assessment has a shadow; the next one is led by that
   shadow under review. Reviews are written, in rounds, with verdicts.
4. **Method and tooling as a shared asset.** The business-function model and the evidence rule;
   the engagement knowledge base; AI agents with read-only tool access to speed up inventory and
   checks. This is also what differentiates us in sales.
5. **Measure the practice.**

   | Metric | Why |
   |---|---|
   | Assessments run; share converted into Blueprint / Implementation | Commercial value |
   | Days from kickoff to first report | Kit effectiveness |
   | People who have led an assessment alone | Succession |
   | Kit reuse: templates used, lessons added per engagement | Whether the kit lives |
   | Client acceptance without rewrite | Quality |
6. **Protect the lead's time.** The practice lead reviews and coaches; execution goes to leads and
   engineers. A request answered by the practice lead personally is a gap in the kit.
